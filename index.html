Loan tips every Kenyan must know

Fuliza ni mtego

At 1% per day, KSh 5,000 on Fuliza for a month costs KSh 1,500 in interest alone — that's 30%! Use it for 1–2 days max.

Build your limit slowly

Borrow small, repay early. M-Shwari and KCB M-Pesa automatically increase your limit for every on-time repayment.

The 20% excise trap

Kenya adds 20% excise duty on all loan interest. A KSh 10,000 loan at 7.5% = KSh 750 interest + KSh 150 tax = KSh 900 total.

Never roll over loans

Taking a new loan to pay an old one doubles your interest. Plan your repayment date the moment you borrow, not after.
